SWK Agribusiness Mentorship Programme 2027

Learn from a mentor. Then take your first steps.

We are building a six-month programme for young people aged 18 to 35: three months learning from people who already run agribusinesses, then three months taking your first steps with their coaching, so you know exactly where to turn and what to do. Tell us where you are and what you need, so we build it around you.
