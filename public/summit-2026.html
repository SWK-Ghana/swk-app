About the Summit

Where ambition meets the soil.

The SWK Ghana Agribusiness Summit 2026 is a free, half-day in-person activation for young people aged 15 to 35 who are curious about agribusiness, already building in it, or looking for the push that turns an idea into a plan. One morning, four phases, and a room full of people going the same way.

It is our first in-person flagship event, and it grows out of something already working. Our webinar series has reached 300+ young people across 9+ countries, proving there is real appetite for practical, unromanticised conversation about food, farming, and enterprise. The Summit takes that online energy and puts it in a room.

This is the founding edition of what will become an annual gathering for young Ghanaian agripreneurs. You will not sit through slides for four hours. You will build something, meet people worth knowing, and leave with a next step you can actually take.

What to Expect

Four phases, one morning.

The programme is built to move: it opens with culture, makes the case, puts tools in your hands, and ends with young people pitching in front of the room.

1

Phase 1

Ignition

A cultural performance and opening that sets the tone and wakes the room up.

The Case

A keynote address, then a rapid-fire panel of working agribusiness voices.

3

Phase 3

The Work

A hands-on workshop where you build your own Agribusiness Model Canvas.

4

Phase 4

The Launch

A youth pitch showcase, then open time to make the connections that last.

Why Attend

Four reasons to be in the room.

Meet the practitioners

Sit with people actually running agribusinesses in Ghana, and ask them the questions the internet cannot answer.

Build your Canvas live

Walk out with an Agribusiness Model Canvas you built yourself, in the room, with guidance on hand.

Pitch, or watch one

Take the mic in the youth pitch showcase, or watch the next generation of agripreneurs make their case.

Join the community

Plug into a growing network of 300+ young agripreneurs across nine countries and counting.
