SWK Agribusiness Mentorship Programme 2027

Help a young person build their agribusiness.

For six months, July to December 2027, each of five mentors guides a circle of four young agripreneurs: three months showing them how a working agribusiness runs, then three months helping them take their first steps and connecting them to the people and services they will need. Selected mentors receive a monthly stipend.

About 5 minutes · 3 short pages Every mentor is verified before matching: identity, business and two references.
