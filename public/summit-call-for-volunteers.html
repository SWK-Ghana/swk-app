SWK Agribusiness Mentorship Programme 2027

Volunteer with the Mentorship Programme.

In 2027, five mentors will guide 20 young agripreneurs for six months: three months learning inside working agribusinesses, then three months taking their first steps. Volunteers make it happen, from finding sponsors and running the orientation to supporting mentees, telling their stories and measuring the results.

About 5 minutes · 3 short pages Volunteering is unpaid. Volunteers who complete their role receive a verifiable SWK certificate.
