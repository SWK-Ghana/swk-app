SWK Ghana Agribusiness Summit 2026 · From the Ground Up

Call for Volunteers

SWK Ghana is inviting enthusiastic, reliable and purpose-driven individuals to volunteer at the Agribusiness Summit 2026 — a half-day youth agribusiness activation designed to Inspire, Equip and Connect young people interested in agriculture and agribusiness.

Saturday 7 November 2026 · 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM The GracedLife Leadership Centre (TGLC), Ashaley Botwe, Accra 50–100 young people expected
Apply to volunteer

About the opportunity

Where volunteers make the day happen.

We are looking for volunteers to support different aspects of the Summit, from welcoming and registering participants to event logistics, media and content, documentation, and monitoring & evaluation. You may apply for one area that matches your interests, skills and experience — final assignments are based on the needs of the Summit and the strengths of selected volunteers.

This is an unpaid volunteer opportunity. Any refreshments, transportation support, or other provisions offered will depend on available resources and will be communicated to selected volunteers in advance.

Administration & RegistrationWelcoming and registering participants on the day.
Event Logistics & Participant SupportSetup, breakdown, and keeping the day running smoothly.
Photography / VideographyCapturing the Summit for our records and channels.
Content CreationLive coverage, reels, and participant testimonial interviews.
Monitoring, Evaluation & DocumentationHelping us capture and learn from the day.
Workshop / Participant SupportSupporting the Model Canvas workshop and pitch showcase.
